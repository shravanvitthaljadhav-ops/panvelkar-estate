🚨 Fire Emergency Assembly Guidance

In the event of a fire or other emergency, residents in and around the designated fire-flat locations should immediately move away from the affected area and proceed to the society's designated fire assembly point, following instructions from society security, the committee or emergency personnel. Do not use lifts during a fire emergency.
